\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

This study combines explicit evidence boundaries with a bounded cold-repair
comparison. Across eight historical families, the semantic-context arm produced
4/16 useful completions versus 5/16 for matched raw context; the descriptive
family-level interval provides no observed advantage for B. The retained
evidence distinguishes useful repairs, executed test failures, incomplete
validation, provider failures, and runtime deviations while preserving the
fixed denominator. Conditional design arguments remain subject to their
trusted premises. Broader repository generalization, human semantic fidelity,
complete cost efficiency, and routing, reuse, or publication benefits require
additional evidence beyond this experiment.
