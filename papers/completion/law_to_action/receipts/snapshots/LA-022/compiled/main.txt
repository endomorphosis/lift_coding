                                                         Abstract
 1             We evaluate a bounded runtime-checking question for tool effects that stand in
 2             for generated-code side effects: under explicit ENFORCE at one pre-invocation
 3             Model Context Protocol (MCP) handler, can an independently observed sandbox
 4             mutation that lacks a matching context-bound admission be prevented, while a
 5             matched admitted case still performs useful permitted work? Legal documents,
 6             CVE-linked vulnerability and repair records, and SkillCenter procedures compile
 7             into Legal, Security, and Intent IR. Those IRs are models with distinct authority,
 8             not proofs of legal correctness, universal vulnerability absence, or permission. On
 9             an admitted 60 × 5 × 3 matrix of frozen modeled-policy probes, full enforcement
10             observed 0/90 policy-forbidden handler effects and completed 90/90 allowed
11             useful work. Unguarded execution and model-free prompt/retrieval labels observed
12             90/90 forbidden effects. Lightweight policy plus UCAN leaked 33/90. Expert
13             legal fidelity, human agreement, and closed-loop model planning were not collected
14             or executed and are withdrawn rather than filled. Closest systems—reference
15             monitors, proof-carrying authorization, Catala, UCAN/Cedar, AgentSpec, Progent,
16             CaMeL, and AgentDojo—motivate implementable arm classes; none is a scored
17             baseline here.


18   1    Introduction
19   An autonomous agent can follow a useful procedure and still violate a legal restriction, reproduce
20   a known vulnerability, or act outside its delegated authority. A safeguard placed only in the model
21   prompt cannot distinguish these cases reliably. The implementation described here places source-
22   grounded constraints and a separate authorization boundary between proposed actions and a protected
23   MCP handler. MCP supplies tool discovery and invocation; application implementations remain
24   responsible for enforcing security requirements [1, 2].
25   The method begins before a tool call. A legal collection supplies normative constraints, a vulnerability-
26   and-fix collection supplies security evidence, and a skill collection supplies descriptions of intended
27   procedures. These become three intermediate representations (IRs), not one undifferentiated retrieval
28   index. Formalization produces typed logic views and proof obligations. The runtime joins the results
29   against one exact invocation, then checks authorization again before the effect. This design combines
30   semantic compilation, proof search, capability security, and stateful execution rather than asking a
31   language model to certify its own behavior.
32   The evaluated workshop contribution is that bounded mediation test [3]. It is not a new authorization
33   logic, a new legal language, or a first agent runtime monitor. AgentSpec, Progent, and CaMeL
34   already supply runtime rules, tool-privilege checks, and capability isolation [4–6]; Catala compiles
35   expert-authored law [7]; AgentDojo measures utility beside attacks [8]. We cite those primary sources
36   as comparison classes. We do not execute them as experimental arms. Source adapters, constraint

42   2   Related work

43   The scored evaluation asks whether an independently observed handler effect is denied at one explicit
44   ENFORCE delegate while matched admitted work remains executable. The comparison below is a
45   literature comparison on that question. No cited external system was executed as a scored arm.

46   Runtime enforcement. Complete mediation requires a check at every access to a protected resource
47   [9]. Security automata and inlined or editing monitors describe which policies a runtime mechanism
48   can enforce [10–13]. Those mechanisms inspect program actions. They do not compile legal or
49   vulnerability sources, and they do not count useful MCP-handler work. AgentSpec supplies agent
50   runtime triggers, predicates, and enforcement rules [4]. Progent checks symbolic tool privileges and
51   classifies policy updates using an SMT solver [5]. These direct precedents rule out claims that this
52   paper introduces agent runtime rules or guarded privilege updates. Neither system was benchmarked
53   here.

54   Proof-carrying admission. Proof-carrying code [14] and proof-carrying authentication [15] re-
55   quire a checker to accept a proof before running untrusted code or granting a request. Translation
56   validation and CompCert separate a translator from a checked proof [16, 17]. A content identifier or
57   a proof_cid in an envelope is not that check.

58   Executable law is not checked derivation. Encoding a statute as a logic program already performs
59   an interpretation [18–20]. Catala compiles literate legal specifications to programs and has been
60   applied to tax law [7, 21]. It is the closest executable-law precedent. It is not an agent runtime. Legal
61   IR in this paper is a bounded, lossy compilation of selected facets; unsupported facets and expert
62   applicability remain labeling problems, not theorems.

63   Capabilities and application-level MCP enforcement. Object capabilities, attenuating caveats,
64   relation tuples, and analyzable policy languages [22–25] justify a lightweight grant check. UCAN
65   specifies signed delegated capabilities [26]. MCP supplies tool invocation; its authorization document
66   specifies OAuth 2.1 resource-server roles and requires implementations to follow OAuth 2.1 security
67   best practices [1, 2]. That protocol is not a generated-code effect monitor. A signature, a CID, or a
68   peer identity is not a generated-code effect observation.

69   Verifiable coding and agent-security evaluations. Functional correctness and issue resolution
70   [27, 28] measure useful coding work without gated forbidden-effect counters. Closed-loop verified
71   generation and autoformalization [29–31] target specs and prover artifacts, not this handler route.
72   AgentDojo, ToolEmu, InjecAgent, τ -bench, and AgentHarm [8, 32–35] measure tool-use attacks,
73   emulated risk, injection, policy following, or harm. CaMeL isolates agent data flow with capabilities
74   [6]. They motivate reporting utility beside forbidden effects. They were not run here.

75   Implementable arms, not imported suites. The matched arms are mechanism classes in this stack:
76   an unguarded handler (A0); inert prompt and retrieval labels as model-free equivalence controls
77   (A1, A2); a qualified policy and capability verifier (A3); and explicit ENFORCE with exact context
78   binding, a qualified proof route, and durable consumption (A4). Prompt or retrieval efficacy is not
79   identified in the model-free study. CVEfixes is a vulnerability/fix collection [36], not a runtime.
80   DuckDB/Quack, IPFS, and libp2p [37–39] are operational substrates. The workshop fit is runtime
81   checking of a proposed effect before a protected mutation [3], not a claim that every cited system was
82   benchmarked.

84    The evaluated source freeze is 30 lineage families and 60 cases: six legal documents, 12 CVE
85    repository families with vulnerable/fixed pairs, and 12 SkillCenter families with a base procedure and
86    an adversarial variant. Physical reads used to reserve that freeze were six legal documents, 4329 rows

89    SkillCenter physical records) remain unrecounted background and are not evaluated populations.
90    Appendix A records family-level provenance requirements. No worldwide-coverage claim follows
91    from the bounded sample.
92    Legal documents compile through a restricted seven-field grammar (modality, actor, action, object,
93    conditions, exceptions, temporal qualifiers) with source maps and declared losses. Compilation is
94    inspectable. Source-to-rule fidelity remains a separate validation requirement and was not expert-
95    reviewed in this study. Executable-law work such as Catala supplies a relevant precedent [7]; it is not
96    an agent runtime or a handler-effect observer.
97    CVEfixes links vulnerability records to source repositories and fixes [36]. The inspected release pins
98    hitoshura25/cvefixes at d4f5c4ea65329d9ccbb8a3b3149e5d06eda5edb2. The adapter constructs
99    a canonical Security IR declaration and rejects wildcard scope and inserted authority grants. Vulnera-
100   ble code is a positive control for a candidate restriction; fixed code is a negative control for that restric-
101   tion. A fix does not certify that all other vulnerabilities are absent. Runtime cve_security_gate
102   maps declared intent and independently extracted handler effects into the same Security IR root.
103   Incomplete mappings remain unknown.
104   SkillCenter supplies procedural Markdown. The bounded reader opens upstream SQLite bundles read-
105   only, disables extension loading, and never executes commands appearing in a skill. The inspected
106   source is Tommysha/skillcenter-bundles at f9dd4fec3c86d85ebf116c7408ac5ce602c418a1. In-
107   tent IR distinguishes goals, guards, effects, and control edges, with source spans and grounded-versus-
108   inferred status. A procedure’s word “permitted” is a source assertion, not a capability. The default
109   skill path is a deterministic structural normalizer. No trained encoder was pinned or evaluated.
110   The three families share identity, provenance, and obligation contracts. They do not share an ontology
111   or grant authority to one another. Sparse, dense, and graph retrieval may nominate evidence; hard
112   applicability and exact identity determine whether it may be used. Retrieval engineering is not a
113   measured token-saving result.

123   An InvocationIntentEnvelope commits to source identity, tenant, actor, audience, tool/version, argu-
124   ments, proposed effects, environment, nonce, and relevant roots. Trusted adapters bind the effect
125   description to the actual operation. The constraint compiler joins Legal, Security, and Intent checks
126   against that exact invocation. A favorable score cannot offset a failed hard constraint.
127   The selected protected route is SupervisorPreInvocationEnforcement.authorize_and_
128   delegate in explicit ENFORCE around BoundedExportHandler.export_json. Independent
129   filesystem-journal counters observe handler effects. A permissible candidate still requires a current
130   grant for the exact actor, tool, resource, and bounds. The runtime revalidates and consumes the
131   permitted use immediately before delegation. Replay, expiry, wrong audience, changed arguments,
132   undeclared effects, and changed roots are denials at that boundary. DuckDB with a typed Quack
133   owner records operational progression for the A4 deployment; the default in-memory store is a
134   labeled contrast only. IPFS names immutable bytes [38]; libp2p carries framed MCP messages [39];
135   UCAN checks signed attenuated delegation [26]. None of those substrates is itself the scored safety
136   result. Kit’s AuthorizationGate is a related policy gate and is not the selected safety claim.


137   5     Evaluation

138   5.1   Frozen protocol and automated evidence scope

139   The predeclared fixed-action study uses 30 source-lineage families and 60 cases (six legal families,
140   12 CVE repository pairs, 12 SkillCenter families), split 6/6/18 into development, calibration, and final
141   by source lineage [36]. Each family contributes one policy-allowed and one policy-forbidden case.
142   Five matched arms share the same candidate identifiers, arguments, and sandbox handler: unguarded
143   execution (A0); inert prompt-text and retrieval-context labels as model-free equivalence controls
144   (A1, A2); lightweight policy plus real Ed25519 UCAN verification (A3); and explicit ENFORCE with
145   exact-context binding, a qualified QF_BOOL SAT job, and file-backed DuckDB consumption (A4).
146   Seeds 104729, 104759, and 104761 yield a 60 × 5 × 3 = 900-cell matrix. The independent unit is the
147   lineage family. Allowed and forbidden labels are the frozen modeled policy and machine-checkable
148   behavior contract, not real-world legality.
149   Outside reviewers were not a run dependency. Expert legal applicability, exception fidelity, semantic
150   accuracy, inter-annotator agreement, and legal validity were not collected and are not scored. Optional
151   author reading, if it occurs later, is non-independent and is not human gold. Generated interpretations
152   remain predictions. The LA-009 skill-validator comparison of 18/18 is shared-producer confor-
153   mance: the same Intent IR decoder and schema functions already invoked by the normalizer, not an
154   independent checker.

215   6     Limitations and modeling assumptions
216   Legal IR is a lossy model of selected facets (modality, actor, action, object, conditions, exceptions,
217   temporal qualifiers), not the law and not a proof of legal correctness. Unsupported constructs must
218   not disappear into a successful admission. Expert legal fidelity, human agreement, and legal-validity
219   rates were not collected; they remain unmeasured rather than filled with compiler agreement or
220   generated labels. Security IR is a scoped candidate prohibition. A CVE or CWE identifier, a fix, or a
221   source-difference observation is not universal vulnerability absence and is not transfer to unrelated
222   code. Skill Markdown is untrusted data; a procedure’s word “permitted” is not a capability.
223   Complete mediation is claimed only for SupervisorPreInvocationEnforcement.authorize_
224   and_delegate in explicit ENFORCE around one benchmark-owned BoundedExportHandler.
225   export_json sandbox mutation. Default OFF is pass-through. The default in-memory consumption
226   store is not the restart-safety configuration; A4 used a file-backed DuckDB typed Quack owner. A
227   DuckDB commit is not remote exactly-once evidence. Unprotected, observational, and nonidentical
228   routes were exercised in qualification and excluded from the safety claim. Local stdio, loopback
229   HTTP, and loopback libp2p parity do not establish wide-area transport.
230   The selected proof route is propositional SAT with an independent truth-table checker. Satisfiability
231   is not a kernel theorem. Compact admitted records do not retain full per-cell proof or capability
232   payloads. The evaluated handler consumes a declarative export instruction; closed-loop model-
233   generated programs were not executed. No trained skill normalizer or encoder was evaluated.
234   Release-card corpus totals were not independently recounted and are not evaluated populations.
235   Optional author review is not a prerequisite for this draft, is not independent annotation, and cannot
236   replace the frozen automated evidence.

237   7     Conclusion
238   On one explicit ENFORCE sandbox route, independently observed forbidden handler effects were
239   prevented for every frozen policy-forbidden probe while matched admitted work still succeeded.
240   Lightweight policy plus UCAN was not sufficient for the residual mutation classes. Unguarded


                                                         6
241   execution and inert prompt or retrieval labels were not sufficient either. The result is a bounded,
242   policy-relative verifiable-coding measurement [3], not superiority to Catala, AgentSpec, Progent,
243   CaMeL, AgentDojo, or any other listed system, and not a claim of legal correctness, expert agreement,
244   or closed-loop generated-program control. Those latter studies remain unmeasured or withdrawn.
245   Optional author review is not required to generate this evidence-scoped draft and would not be
246   independent human validation.
