\section{Limitations}

The eight recruited families form a convenience sample. Two repetitions within
a family do not create additional independent repositories, and the repetition
identifiers were not sent to the model API as random seeds. The frozen
family bootstrap gives descriptive uncertainty conditional on this sample;
it cannot establish broad population coverage, superiority, or noninferiority.
Adding semantic context also changes the complete request, so the comparison
cannot attribute an effect to an individual semantic component or to prompt
compression alone.

Finite public and hidden checks cover their actual assertions. A collection
failure supplies no observation of assertions that never executed, and passing
the available checks does not establish complete behavioral correctness.
Independent human semantic fidelity, expert agreement, and human time were not
measured. AI inspection of candidate bytes is a procedural check and supplies
none of those missing measurements.

The broader original experiment was reduced before final outcomes. The
preserved 192-cell plan contains 160 withdrawn factor cells; eight additional
target families were not recruited. Component ablations, C/D comparisons,
warm-cache reuse, and publication efficacy remain unmeasured. Source-present
components and earlier constructed integration witnesses retain their own
scope and cannot fill those gaps. The conditional design arguments also leave
dependency completeness, checker adequacy, authorized write-path enforcement,
and uncontrolled external effects as explicit obligations.

Execution spans an original runtime and an admitted continuation after a lease
interruption. Provider errors, actual timer overruns, and the interruption stay
in the record. Child, wrapper, gateway, scorer, and operator clocks can overlap;
their values are not a disjoint total. Shared preparation is accounted for
once in its stated scope, earlier failures remain separate, and unknown remote
charges and unmeasured author effort remain unknown. These restrictions limit
claims of net efficiency even when a repair meets the useful-completion rule.

Anonymous numerical reproduction has a narrower scope than repeating the
original service execution or rescoring confidential oracles. A derivative
with identifying metadata removed is bound to its original digest but is not
authenticated by the original signature on different bytes. Public reference
source derivatives are also distinguished from executed source. Stable hashes
and upstream attribution can permit correlation with other material, so a
lexical anonymity check supplies a bounded audit, not a guarantee of
de-identification.
