                                                         Abstract
 1             Coding agents repeatedly reconstruct repository state, regenerate plans, and re-
 2             run validation, yet shorter prompts or cached passes can omit dependencies and
 3             weaken assurance. We present an obligation-centered architecture that maintains
 4             content-addressed semantic state separately from operational task state and durable
 5             evidence. A formalization layer maps code, intent, and applicable constraints to
 6             typed obligations while exposing unsupported or lossy translations. A supervisor
 7             uses these obligations to select deterministic analysis, compositional reasoning,
 8             proof search, bounded synthesis, or an explicitly authorized neural residual. Candi-
 9             date edits are checked against current source; test and proof results are admitted
10             only for their declared statements, environments, and trust profiles. Accepted
11             transitions update durable roots and invalidate affected planning and verification
12             state. The design includes incremental proof-unit sealing and staged fixture-aware
13             test reuse, semantic-world memory, and procedural synthesis. We specify separate
14             tests of preservation, progress, and end-to-end cost, including matched raw-context
15             baselines, cold validation oracles, and positive authorization cases. [RESULTS:
16             insert the frozen task population, useful completion rate, net measured cost, un-
17             certainty intervals, and false-admission results.] The contribution is a method for
18             governing repository evolution with explicit evidence, not a claim that hashing,
19             model confidence, or every available solver proves arbitrary Python correct.


20   1 Problem and contribution
21   The cost of an agent is not only the tokens used to generate a patch. It includes rediscovering relevant
22   code, reconstructing assumptions, choosing tests, recovering interrupted work, and deciding whether
23   previous evidence still applies. Repository agents and automated testing studies make the quality of
24   the agent–tool boundary central to useful repair [8, 11]. Here, that boundary is a persistent state-and-
25   obligation representation rather than an unstructured conversation history. Our proposed method has
26   three linked contributions. First, it compiles source and domain intent into identity-bound semantic
27   evidence and explicit unresolved obligations. Second, it makes planning, counterexample-guided
28   repair, and procedural synthesis consumers of those obligations, retaining neural models only for
29   unresolved proposals. Third, it separates test observations, proof statements, reuse decisions, and
30   accepted state transitions, enabling incremental work without silently upgrading evidence. Semantic
31   context reduction is one measurable consequence of this loop, not its sole purpose. The prototype
32   contains source-present components and incompletely qualified integration paths. This working paper
33   distinguishes those from methods specified in active workstreams and from results actually measured.

49   2 State and evidence are typed, separate authorities
50   2.1 The persistent state model

51   Let F be a frozen repository forest, including source trees, declared overlays, dependency pins, and
52   relevant environment identities. The incremental semantic index extracts stable symbols, semantic
53   versions, typed relationships, and limitations. A separate semantic-state producer seals the finalized
54   index into immutable facts, links, capsules, and environment bindings. It supplies pure selection and
55   freshness operations; it does not execute tests, operate a task queue, or decide that a candidate is
56   accepted. [D1]

57   2.2 Claims carry provenance and applicability

58   The domain-neutral IR kernel separates declarations, runs, results, and receipts. A declaration does
59   not acquire a different semantic identity because a model or solver ran. Canonical identities bind a
60   schema/domain and canonicalization profile; operational roots can reference declarations and their
61   histories without inserting attempts, clocks, or leases into semantic content. Legal, Security, and
62   Intent families share these protocols but do not share verdict authority or ontology. [N4]

 94   3 Formalization: preserve the property before proving it
 95   3.1 Domain intent is not execution authority

 96   A high-level objective is first a candidate specification. Intent IR expresses goals, actions, resources,
 97   and effects; Legal IR contributes applicable normative constraints; Security IR contributes principal,
 98   resource, and effect invariants. Their shared IR kernel supplies canonical claims and evidence
 99   references, not an automatic equivalence between domains. Reviewed formalization and source
100   mappings turn a candidate into bounded obligations; ambiguity, missing coverage, and unsupported
101   modalities remain explicit. [N4–N7] The attested authorization design queries a manifest-bound proof
102   corpus, checks applicability and revocation, composes obligations, and produces an exact-context
103   decision. Correctness and authoriza- tion are separate gates. The inspected reference enforcement
104   layer supports one-time consumption and context rechecks, but does not itself connect to real tools;
105   the documented receipt-consumption rollout defaults off/shadow. Thus the paper claims an interface
106   and mechanism only at the deployment stage actually exercised. [N7, N11]

107   3.2 Loss-aware translation and solver-state limits

108   A target formula is useful only for claims preserved by its translation. For example, a legacy
109   projection removes temporal/deontic operators and approximates a binary temporal formula by
110   conjunction. A proof of that projection cannot prove the original obligation. The admitted route
111   must reject unsupported loss or bind an independently justified preservation relation. Translation
112   validation checks the particular transformation rather than trusting every conversion [4]. [N8] The
113   inspected incremental SMT wrapper maintains named assertions and replay identity but rebuilds
114   a Z3 solver per check; that path supplies no exported proof object. It therefore supports assertion-
115   level incrementality, not an established retained-clause speedup. Solver diversity, same-solver core
116   rechecking, native proof reconstruction, and kernel checking must be reported separately. [D2]
117   The actual component checker lowers a producer guarantee G and consumer assumption A to G ∧
118   ¬A; SAT supplies a countermodel, UNSAT discharges the encoded implication, and unsupported
119   clauses remain uncovered. The bounded QF_LIA CEGAR adapter searches a Boolean abstraction,
120   concretizes an error trace, and refines only spurious traces using validated predicates. A feasible trace
121   refutes the model; incomplete search is not a proof. These results become reasoned plan obligations
122   and localized repair inputs. [N12–N13]

123   4 Planning and state repair before neural execution
124   4.1 Objective compilation and plan admission

125   The direct-objective workstream replaces manual prompt elaboration with a bounded compiler:
126   authorized input and repository scan yield goals, subgoals, task dependencies, assumptions, permitted
127   effects, exact outputs, and acceptance obligations. Deterministic templates cover known task families.
128   A model may propose a residual decomposition, but the result must pass schema, dependency, scope,
129   resource, and acceptance-coverage checks. An inferred specification is still a proposal until admitted;
130   a model must not lower the objective or remove a required test to make its own plan succeed. [W2;
131   DOEP] Planning maintains an obligation frontier. Resolved obligations reference current evidence;
132   unre- solved obligations carry a reason and a permissible next route. Hard authority, safety, and
133   effect conditions are checked before ranking by cost. The intended routing policy selects the cheapest
134   adequate route, not a ritual invocation of every theorem prover.

141   4.2 A routing gate is not a proof kernel

142   The pre-implementation kernel can defer unavailable capabilities, choose a unique analytical can-
143   didate, abstain on ambiguous candidates, or authorize a named neural residual using resolved task/
144   forest-bound receipts. It does not run a model, prove a candidate, or by itself complete a task. A
145   Boolean “closes claim” on a candidate requires independent downstream justification. Its local zero
146   provider-hook count is not a measurement of saved model calls across the workflow. [A1, N1]
147   Integration is part of the method. The real caller must bind the current plan, diagnostic view and
148   obligation graph to the same task and forest as its evidence. A valid authorized residual must progress
149   while a minimally altered invalid request fails. Measuring only rejection would reward a deny-all
150   agent rather than useful neurosymbolic control.

151   4.3 Event-driven reassessment and bounded convergence

152   Repository, dependency, policy, proof, fixture, capability, and owner events invalidate only the
153   affected causal/semantic frontier when that frontier is complete. The supervisor retains accepted
154   unaffected subplans and emits a PlanDelta rather than rebuilding an entire prompt. Refill creates
155   only tasks needed for explicit gaps or counterexamples, subject to plan epochs, generation limits,
156   repeated-candidate detection, and authority. Unknown provider effects enter reconciliation rather
157   than blind retry. [W2; DOEP] Queue exhaustion is not success. Completion requires current evidence
158   covering every mandatory acceptance obligation and no unresolved required consumer. Bounded
159   termination is also not con- vergence to a solution: budget exhaustion, nonconvergence, unavailable
160   tools, and human decisions remain separate terminal outcomes. Incoming world-model self-hosting
161   is qualified only when route and acceptance records show actual consumption of state, not merely
162   sidecar logging. [W1–W2]

163   5 Repair, context compilation, and procedural synthesis
164   5.1 Localize and preserve the intended change

165   A repair begins with a state-bound failed obligation, observation, or counterexample—not a free-form
166   request to “fix everything.” Static dependency analysis and abstract interpretation bound the affected
167   region. Assume–guarantee checks expose unmet component interfaces; validated unsat cores or
168   interpolants can identify a compact missing premise. CEGAR refines an abstraction after a spurious
169   counterexample; CEGIS refines candidate implementations against accumulated counterexamples [3,
170   5]. Conflating these loops would obscure whether the program or its abstraction changed. Candidate
171   plans, patch sketches, and CodeEditPackets remain non-authoritative. The currently represented path
172   checks preimages and scope, applies a Git patch in an isolated fenced worktree, rescans semantic
173   state, and runs selected plus required fallback validation. General tokenizer-alias IR editing and exact
174   CST/AST relinking remain separate PCSM extensions until their actual resolver, renderer, round-trip
175   and adversarial checks are frozen. A source map alone is not a proof of semantic preservation.

176   5.2 Context minimizes unresolved information, not obligations

177   The semantic compiler owns semantic ContextPack identity and sufficiency; the runtime compiles
178   provider-facing ContextCapsules. The goal, authority, scope, and acceptance core is non-truncatable.
179   Optional evidence is chosen under byte/token budgets; exclusions have exact expansion references
180   and reason codes. Required target source, caveats, tests, and proof obligations cannot be removed
181   to hit a compression target. Parent-bound delta capsules carry new counterexamples or dependency
182   facts while retaining the invariant core. Stable prefixes and provider cache reuse remain bound to
183   model/tokenizer/policy identities. [D1; ASEH, PCSM] A model request names the residual question,
184   admitted evidence, missing information, budget, expected response schema, and independent accep-
185   tance test. Failed candidates expand only implicated source or contracts when possible. This adapts

188   5.3 Compile repeated successful work into bounded operators

189   ProcedureCegis already implements a bounded synthesis state machine. Its source order is existing
190   verified procedure, built-in template, anti-unified pattern, declarative/constraint candidate, model
191   sketch, and human candidate. Candidate and counterexample-set identities prevent repeated evaluation
192   of the same pair. The synthesizer neither executes nor certifies nor promotes its output. [A2] The
193   incoming memory method mines independently admitted trajectories, preserves failed episodes,
194   infers applicability conditions, and validates anti-unified procedures on held-out families. Each
195   procedure has typed parameters, effects, branch/loop bounds, residual holes, rollback, and required
196   validation. Execution must recheck current applicability and authority. Learned call-target, event-
197   delta, inverse-trace, and repair-delta models rank constrained candidates or abstain; their predicted
198   transitions become accepted state only after observation or verification. [W2, W4] Architecture
199   refactoring applies the same loop to module boundaries and state ownership: SCC-based candidates,
200   compatibility contracts, source-preserving edits, differential traces, tests/proofs, and rollback. A
201   smaller dependency graph, fewer files, or lower context burden only ranks candidates; it does not
202   establish equivalence. [W4]


203   6 Test certificates and sound conditions for reuse

204   6.1 Specify exactly what is certified

205   A test certificate must name its statement, source/dependency roots, runner and environment, fixture
206   inputs, result semantics, and checker/trust profile. “Verified” is not a single universal evidence class.
207   A manifest can be complete while its child observations depend on a trusted runner; a circuit can
208   correctly verify signatures while proving nothing about honest execution by their signer. [K1, A5]
209   The source-present Groth16 adapter is gated by explicit opt-in and a compiled Rust backend, with
210   BN254 and versioned circuit references. Its inspected derivation helper accepts only propositional
211   atoms, facts, and one-antecedent implications, using forward chaining. This is not the full temporal/
212   deontic system and not a CPython/pytest execution model. Versioned wrapper code, setup availability,
213   and attestation metadata must not be mistaken for a qualified circuit/key ceremony or measured
214   prover performance. [N9–N10]

228   The PCTDD workstream separates collection seed, fixture-definition closure, materialized fixture-

235   6.3 Exact reuse and cross-tree reuse are distinct

236   A full-tree cache key changes on an unrelated edit. Broader reuse therefore needs a current dependency
237   projection and a noninterference/applicability witness, not just removal of the tree from a key. If
238   a deterministic observation under profile θ is a function fθ of a complete projection πD, then
239   πD(s)=πD(s′) implies equal observations. This conditional argument is elementary; proving that πD
240   includes all relevant inputs and effects is the hard obligation. Runtime traces alone cannot show that
241   no unobserved dependency exists. [W3; Appendix I]


242   7 Incremental proof state and durable acceptance

243   7.1 Completeness is independent of membership

244   Verification is partitioned into source-addressed proof units with stable logical identities and complete
245   applicability/trust keys. A requirement manifest declares every mandatory unit; a proof forest binds
246   the evidence for those units in deterministic categories. Merkle membership proves inclusion, not
247   that the collector included all required tests. Selection completeness and authorized removal of
248   obligations are separately checked. Delta seals name the accepted parent and exact reused, replaced,
249   added and removed units; uncertainty forces broader verification or a full checkpoint. [A5, K1]

250   7.2 Prepared artifacts cannot publish themselves

251   The durable store persists canonical blocks and candidate evidence; the supervisor runtime decides
252   whether evidence satisfies current obligations. The small persistence adapter and actual pointer CAS
253   are separate surfaces. Current-seal publication checks repository/branch identity, expected parent and
254   generation. Local locks, atomic filesystem publication, and WAL recovery do not constitute global
255   distributed consensus or a transaction spanning Git, the task database, and remote services. Crash
256   recovery must explicitly reconcile those boundaries. [K1–K4]

276   7.4 Multi-agent and world-state extension

277   Sibling supervisors exchange typed requests, observations, and receipts, not writable access to
278   each other’s state. Mergeable observational indexes can nominate dependencies; exclusive claims,
279   fences, policy, publication, and completion remain transactional. Accepted world roots can compose
280   semantic, program-trace, logical-dataset, procedure, proof, and environment roots. Cyclic logical
281   graphs use stable IDs and snapshot manifests rather than literal cyclic content links. Required-mode
282   operation must exhibit pre-state consumption, accepted transition evidence, and post-state publication.

345   9 Evidence limits, related work, and conclusion
346   9.1 What the existing artifacts establish

347   The reviewed standalone repository heads and the consumer’s dependency pins differ. A final claim
348   must bind the actual loaded source forest, including overlays, native binaries, solver/circuit profiles
349   and active rollout settings. Source-present intent authorization and a Groth16 wrapper must not
350   be described as always-on enforcement or a validated universal prover. The preceding integration
351   findings belong in the author audit until resolved or reported as limitations.

352   9.2 Relationship to established methods

353   Proof-carrying code attaches checkable policy evidence to untrusted code [1]; abstract interpretation
354   and CEGAR reason over approximations and refine them with counterexamples [2, 3]. Translation
355   validation checks a produced transformation, and CEGIS searches bounded candidates against
356   counterexamples [4, 5]. Equality saturation represents alternatives under rewrite relations [6]. These
357   are foundations, not newly invented algorithms in this work. The intended difference is composition
358   at the agent’s operational boundary: persistent semantic state, explicit obligations, residual model
359   authorization, phase-aware test evidence, and accepted- transition publication. Build-system research
360   separates scheduling from rebuilding [7]; our reuse design additionally exposes statement, fixture,
361   trust and authority conditions. Coding agents and test-generation benchmarks [8, 11], premise-
362   retrieval and hammer systems [9, 10], and prompt compression [12] provide relevant baselines. We
363   do not claim that this is the first neurosymbolic agent or that combining these techniques automatically
364   improves performance.

365   9.3 Limitations and accountable self-improvement

366   Python reflection, native code, external state, concurrency, incomplete specifications, and unsound
367   translations limit what can be proved. A sound checker cannot repair an incorrectly formalized
368   requirement. Default-deny behavior can mask broken positive paths. A self-improving supervisor
369   must not edit its own protected acceptance specification, keys, validators or promotion policy under
370   ordinary task authority. Every learned update or compiled procedure requires an independent holdout
371   and rollback gate. Important model use in implementation, formalization, candidate generation,
372   experiments and manuscript preparation must be disclosed accurately. The final claims remain
373   conditional on the frozen evidence and tested deployment. Our thesis is that persistent obligations can
374   displace repeated neural reasoning and validation while preserving a declared acceptance standard;
375   the paired evaluation must determine whether the total benefit exceeds the added analysis and proof
376   cost.

598   D Semantic objects, projection indexes and logical datasets
599   D.1 Semantic objects and relations

600   Wrap existing domain-state CIDs in adjacent versioned semantic-object envelopes containing domain,
601   schema, canonicalization profile, provenance, source CIDs, optional declared policy salt and evidence
602   class. Do not alter the existing source-semantic root to include embeddings, prompts, actors, leases,
603   wall time or transition history. Pure semantic deltas remain distinct from operational execution
604   records. [W1, W2] Relations are scoped claims such as equivalent, refines, entails, contradicts,
605   compatible_with and supersedes. Each binds left/right objects, scope, assumptions, environment
606   and theory/policy, proof/ evidence references, limitations and invalidation dependencies. “Similar”
607   belongs to a retrieval projection, not the authoritative semantic relation vocabulary. Exact CID
608   equality establishes byte identity, not universal semantic equivalence. [W1]

609   D.2 Hybrid retrieval without authority leakage

610   Projection records bind their semantic object, projection kind, model, tokenizer, preprocessor, dimen-
611   sion, metric, normalization, vector identity and privacy policy. Rebuildable index manifests bind
612   generations and model/projection compatibility. Lexical, vector, LSH/Hamming, graph or neural
613   search may nominate candidates. The resolver must retrieve exact object bytes, verify current root
614   membership and recheck freshness, environment, obligations and policy before use. A stale vector
615   or approximate neighbor never substitutes for an exact subject. [W1, W2] The graph-to-graph
616   or typed-attention TAGSeq/DAGSeq idea remains an optional experimental projection over typed
617   program neighborhoods. It must be compared with exact lookup, rules, ordinary graph features and
618   embedding-only baselines. Canonical symbols and typed source maps remain authoritative even if
619   the learned projection changes. [W2]

620   D.3 Logical data versus physical layout

621   LogicalDatasetState and PhysicalDatasetLayout must not collapse into one identity. Under a fixed
622   logical schema, key/order policy and provenance, changing Parquet row groups, compression or shard
623   packaging should preserve logical content identity while changing layout identity. Set, multiset and
624   ordered semantics must be explicit; duplicate and key-conflict behavior cannot be inferred from file
625   order. [W1] Transformations bind exact inputs, transformation specification, environment/toolchain,
626   candidate outputs and validation evidence. Merges use a closed declared semantics—append, keyed
627   three-way, ordered, set/multiset, schema evolution or conflict. Distributed workers may prepare
628   shards and candidate layouts, but only accepted logical state and validated provenance may enter a
629   world root. Legacy or mock dataset managers cannot silently remain production semantic authorities.
630   [W1, W2]

946   I.3 What can be proved about acceptance and reuse

947   I.3.1 Accepted-lineage invariant — proof sketch, not machine checked

948   Fix a statement profile Θ and a policy predicate PΘ over artifact manifests. Assume: the initial
949   accepted root satisfies PΘ; all required subject bytes and dependency references are verified; the
950   manifest is complete under an admitted completeness rule; each accepted evidence item satisfies its
951   designated checker relation; the sole publication operation enforces the expected parent and all gates;
952   and protected policy/checker identities cannot change under worker authority.
953   Then every root reachable solely through accepted publication transitions satisfies PΘ. Proof:
954   induction on the number of successful publications. The initial case is assumed. For the induction
955   step, the publisher checks PΘ on the exact candidate manifest and current parent before its CAS
956   linearization; failed or stale attempts are not successors. The induction does not establish any property
957   omitted by PΘ, truth of unsupported translations, honest signed observations, or atomicity of external
958   side effects.


959   I.3.2 Dependency-projected test reuse — proof obligation

960   Let Obsθ(t,s) be the relevant setup/call/teardown observation of test t in state s under a declared
961   runtime/effect profile θ. Suppose a complete projection πD and deterministic function fθ exist
962   such that Obsθ(t,s)=fθ(πD(s)) on all states admitted by the reuse policy. If πD(s)=πD(s′), equality
963   of the observations follows immediately. An admitted previous observation can therefore transfer
964   only within that profile and those assumptions. The difficult part is establishing completeness
965   and determinism of πD. Definitions alone miss fixture values; function coverage misses plugin,
966   clock, network, DB and teardown effects. Two equal digests of incomplete manifests do not satisfy
967   the premise. For effectful tests the profile must also show that required effects are replayed or
968   observationally unnecessary. Otherwise run setup and/or the test and teardown. A cold oracle and
969   adversarial mutations check the implementation but do not prove universal closure.


970   I.3.3 Three acceptance questions, three different answers

971   Behavioral claim: does candidate program/model satisfy its obligation?
972   Action permission: may this actor perform this action now?
973   Publication: does this candidate and its evidence become current?
974   Neither a proof of behavior nor a capability alone answers all three. Formalization determines
975   the statement; authorization binds actor, tool, arguments, resources, effects, scope, time and roots;
976   execution observations report what happened; publication chooses the accepted lineage. The reference
977   pre-dispatch code uses an in-memory consumption ledger and fake-dispatch defaults, so durable
978   real-tool integration requires its own adapter and restart evidence. [N7, N11]


979   I.3.4 Useful autonomy requires positive-path witnesses

980   Require a paired witness for each gate: a correctly authorized, well-bound case that progresses and a
981   minimally altered case that is rejected. Include a deny-all control in safety/cost diagnostics, not as a
982   competitive repair method. Otherwise default deferral, unavailable backends or adapter mismatches
983   can masquerade as perfect safety and low inference cost.

1056   L How symbolic computations change the next agent action
1057   L.1 Abstract state supplies candidate bounds

1058   The inspected Python abstract interpreter uses a product of flat constants, integer intervals, nullness,
1059   exceptions and effects. Its immutable lattice protocol exposes order, join, meet, widening and
1060   narrowing; the adapter records local interprocedural summaries and source positions. The worklist
1061   accumulates possible states at program points. Unsupported reflection, native calls, loading and
1062   callbacks widen to opacity. A consumer may prune an impossible candidate only under a sound
1063   admitted approximation, never from a learned low score. [N14]

                     Abstract state = Const × Interval × Nullness × Exceptions × Effects.                  (4)

1064   L.2 Interface composition creates a precise discharge job

1065   A CompositionEdge references exact producer/consumer contracts and named normal, exceptional
1066   and induction clauses. Unknown clause references are rejected before solver execution. The compo-
1067   sition graph and receipt bind the semantic root, contracts, edges, obligation outcomes, uncovered
1068   assumptions and SCCs. Its basic implication query is G ∧ ¬A. SAT provides a countermodel;
1069   UNSAT provides solver-backed discharge of that encoded implication. Opaque prose stays uncovered.
1070   Cycles need a typed invariant plus an independently solved closure check rather than mutually citing
1071   guarantees. [N12]

                          G ∧ ¬A is UNSAT         ⇒     G⇒A       (in the encoded theory).                 (5)
1072   This permits a caller to consume a dependency guarantee instead of repeatedly loading its body only
1073   when current bindings, statement coverage and the consumer assumptions hold. A contract
1074   gap becomes a named repair or proof task. Consistency and reachability checks prevent treating an
1075   impossible guarantee as meaningful implementation correctness.

1076   L.3 CEGAR localizes abstraction error before code repair

1077   The QF_LIA adapter searches a supplied Boolean abstraction of a control-flow graph and constructs
1078   a named concrete path formula for each abstract error trace. A feasible path is retained as a

1079   real counterexample. A spurious path may be refined with a validated interpolant/core, weakest
1080   precondition, or reviewed predicate. Iteration, predicate, abstract-state and trace-length budgets
1081   are explicit; unavailable, unknown, timeout and budget exhaustion are separate dispositions. It is a
1082   refinement adapter over existing contracts, not a second unrestricted model checker. [N13]

1097   Algorithm 1: Intended composed supervision loop. Input: authorized q, current source forest F, policy
1098   P, budget B
1099   1. reconcile event, task and artifact state; refresh F and derive G, O
1100   2. if cancelled or budget exhausted: return explicit non-success
1101   3. if every current obligation is admitted: return verify_goal_completion()
1102   4. generate bounded task proposals; preview and separately admit ready work
1103   5. if no task is admissible: return wait_or_review(), not goal success
1104   6. claim task; bind lease/fence, preimages, required context and validation
1105   7. choose deterministic, authorized residual-model, review or unavailable route
1106   8. if review/unavailable: record blocked attempt; return to the event service
1107   9. produce and apply candidate only through checked isolated execution
1108   10. rescan; derive O_new from authorized rules, not the worker’s pass claim
1109   11. verify/reuse evidence; retain failed, unknown and pending obligations
