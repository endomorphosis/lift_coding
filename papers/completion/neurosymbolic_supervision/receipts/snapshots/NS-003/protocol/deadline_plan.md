# Deadline-aware, non-submitting plan — NS-003

This plan records a safe decision sequence, not a confirmed portal status or authorization to submit. The review recorded a 2026 workshop CFP check on 2026-09-11 with *tentative* dates of abstract 2026-09-11 AoE, paper 2026-09-13 AoE, and camera-ready 2026-10-14. Those dates, portal availability, template rules, artifact limits, and disclosure requirements must be rechecked against the live official CFP/portal by an authorized human before packaging. No task in this plan sends, uploads, publishes, registers, or submits anything.

## Time-pressure rules

1. A deadline never authorizes a weaker population, changed oracle, dropped failures, invented author facts, or a new optional implementation campaign.
2. Pilot data may repair protocol/runner defects before D2. Final outcomes may not decide the task selection, metrics, thresholds, denominators, or acceptance criteria.
3. If the portal date arrives before D5/D6, the truthful closure is **not ready for submission** (or a separately author-approved, explicitly narrowed non-efficacy manuscript after claim reconciliation). It is never a positive result by absence of a queue.
4. D0 is repeated immediately before any human packaging decision because the recorded CFP dates are tentative and portal conditions may change. A local date, cached web page, or task receipt is not proof that submission is open or authorized.
5. The sole terminal handoff of this track is a reviewed package and an explicit decision record for an authorized author. Human consent, identity, model-use disclosure, artifact rights, and submit action remain external facts to be confirmed rather than guessed.

## Minimal critical path

`D1 → D2 → D3 → D5 → D6` is the evidence path for a comparative core claim. `D4` runs alongside it and closes optional extensions truthfully; it must not block D3/D5 merely because a prover, learned model, or federation backend is unavailable. The preparation sequence is deliberately not a promise that the present deadline can be met.
