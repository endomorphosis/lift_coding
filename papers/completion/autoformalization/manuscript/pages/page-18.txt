556   The three feedback loops. The representation loop changes shared weights. The program-
557   improvement loop changes a compiler/decompiler or its admitted guidance under tests and source-
558   fidelity checks. The proof-assistance loop learns which obligation, premise, or route may help from
559   trusted verifier feedback. They exchange diagnostics and typed artifacts, but their parameters, targets
560   and acceptance criteria are not identical.

561   I     Dataset construction and supervision provenance
562   A legal training example records citation, raw text, normalized text, embedding producer, vector,
563   parsed modal document, frame candidates/selection, parser trace and per-example loss records. The
564   builder normalizes text, obtains a deterministic parse, and ranks frames. Its default vector is a seeded
565   mock suitable for fixtures; the local codec supplies a different, spaCy-feature-hashing route, while a
566   caller may supply another explicit producer. Every dataset release should preserve the distinction
567   rather than silently labeling all vectors pretrained semantic embeddings.
568   The initial IR is therefore often teacher-generated supervision. Family proportions summarize opera-
569   tors already in the parse. Multi-view distributions are generated by registered adapters and configured
570   contract/routing signals, including explicit floors, caps and source-cue rules in the inspected builder. A
571   low loss against those targets demonstrates learning those associations; source truth requires separate
572   annotation. A failed syntax or unavailable prover result must retain its status instead of receiving an
573   invented successful training label.
