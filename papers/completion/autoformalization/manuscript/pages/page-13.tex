\section*{B  Preservation contract and proof-transfer argument}
\noindent
Let SB be the source models admitted under explicit bridge assumptions B. Let \ensuremath{\tau} be fixed on a\linebreak[4]
specified premise and goal language. The contract is not required to cover every source sentence.\linebreak[4]
Proposition 1 (Conditional semantic consequence transfer). Suppose for every s \ensuremath{\in} SB satisfying\linebreak[4]
\ensuremath{\Gamma}, there exists a target model t such that RB (s, t) holds. Suppose related models preserve premises\linebreak[4]
in the direction of Equation 7 and reflect the goal in the direction of Equation 8. If a sound target\linebreak[4]
checker establishes \ensuremath{\tau} (\ensuremath{\Gamma}) |= \ensuremath{\tau} (\ensuremath{\phi}), then \ensuremath{\Gamma} |=SB \ensuremath{\phi}.\par

\noindent
Proof. Take any s \ensuremath{\in} SB satisfying \ensuremath{\Gamma}. Existence supplies a related t. Premise preservation gives\linebreak[4]
t |= \ensuremath{\tau} (\ensuremath{\Gamma}). Target consequence gives t |= \ensuremath{\tau} (\ensuremath{\phi}). Goal reflection gives s |= \ensuremath{\phi}. The argument holds for\linebreak[4]
each such s.\par

\noindent
This is a paper-level conditional argument, not a machine-checked theorem of the repository. It\linebreak[4]
does not establish that the source extraction was faithful, that B was appropriate, that the model\linebreak[4]
class was nonempty, or that a legacy converter satisfies the two preservation directions. Test consis-\linebreak[4]
tency/reachability separately to avoid reporting a vacuous result as practical assurance.\par

\noindent
Suggested translation receipt. The following is specification notation rather than a newly imple-\linebreak[4]
mented replacement for existing contracts:\par

\noindent
source-view, target-view, source-formula-root,\linebreak[4]
target-formula-root,\linebreak[4]
signature-map, interpretation-profile, bridge-assumptions,\linebreak[4]
preserved-property, preservation-direction,\linebreak[4]
supported-fragment,\linebreak[4]
named-losses, source-map-root, checker-environment,\linebreak[4]
checker-result, proof-or-countermodel-reference, status\par

\noindent
A consuming query must check that the receipt's preserved property and direction are adequate for that\linebreak[4]
query. Equi-satisfiability alone cannot establish a mapping of every model's temporal observations.\linebreak[4]
A loss flag cannot be ignored because a later solver returned UNSAT.\par

\noindent
Multi-source joins. For a document actor, code parameter, and event field, include a reviewed\linebreak[4]
alignment claim rather than collapsing all matching strings. Bind namespace, tenant, instance identity,\linebreak[4]
role, validity interval, source authority, and resolution method. Time bridges must specify clock origin,\linebreak[4]
monotonicity, calendar/duration semantics, timezone where relevant, and unit conversion. Missing\linebreak[4]
evidence remains unresolved. The result should retain all bridge assumptions so that revocation or\linebreak[4]
source changes can invalidate dependent conclusions.\par

\noindent
Contradictory sources. Classical theorem provers may derive any goal from inconsistent premises.\linebreak[4]
Do not repair inconsistency by suppressing an inconvenient observation or promoting a model's\linebreak[4]
confidence. Partition contexts, identify conflicting premises, request authoritative resolution, or\linebreak[4]
choose a separately justified inconsistency-tolerant semantics. Such a change is a logic-profile change\linebreak[4]
and must not be hidden inside a translation adapter.\par
