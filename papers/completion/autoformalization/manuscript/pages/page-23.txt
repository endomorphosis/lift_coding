735   O.1   Planning is deterministic until explicitly guided

736   The local LogicTactician validates the goal and policy, binds the configuration identity, validates
737   and partitions candidate sources, optionally applies pinned guidance, selects routes under budgets, and
738   decomposes declared proof gaps. Cycles cause abstention rather than a malformed plan. Excluded
739   sources retain a reason, and final plans carry input roots and guidance identities. The planner’s own
740   implementation performs no proof, write, or network work. [artifact S38]
741   This is a domain-neutral orchestration layer. Its name should not be taken to imply implementation of
742   an external tactic-learning system. A separate executor is needed to realize a plan, and that execution
743   must supply its own artifacts and outcomes. Optional ranking and nomination have a narrower
744   contract than unconstrained generation of new assumptions or arbitrary executable tasks.
