          Table 1: Measurements needed to distinguish learning from copying or teacher self-consistency.

      Question                Required comparison
      Generalization          Sample-memory on/off; disjoint final test set; supervised target provenance
      Reconstruction          Embedding MSE/cosine versus source-withheld text and independently
                              labeled IR facets
      Multi-view learning     Per-family/view CE and coverage; modal/exception/quantifier minimal pairs
      Proof feedback          Heads off/on; calibration, abstention, actual checker outcomes,
                              protected-objective invariance
      Compiler transfer       No guidance versus admitted guidance; fixed-canary and held-out fidelity;
                              rollback
      Economics               Compilation, target construction, gradient updates, validation, search, retries,
                              and provider cost
