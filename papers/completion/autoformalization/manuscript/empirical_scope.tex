\paragraph{Empirical scope.}
We evaluate automated structural and execution properties of the inspected
pipeline: typed elaboration, declared compiler contracts, reconstruction
agreement, training/checkpoint updates, and native checker acceptance of exact
formal goals under stated assumptions. Each reported quantity must be supported
by its own execution record, population, seed, source and resource bindings.
A checker-accepted formal statement is not evidence that the statement faithfully
captures the original natural-language source. Automatic teacher agreement and
round-trip agreement are reported as such.
\paragraph{Unperformed human evaluation.}
No independent human semantic-fidelity study, inter-annotator agreement study,
or adjudication was performed for this manuscript. Their proposed protocols
are retained as unexecuted work and supply no empirical score. Any optional
author comments, if supplied, are descriptive non-independent feedback and are
not evaluation gold. The manuscript's supported contribution is limited to the
automated evidence actually reported; source-semantic fidelity and its transfer
remain unmeasured. Development, fixed-canary and constructed results must not
be presented as untouched final-distribution performance.
