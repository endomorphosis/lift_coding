683   N     Retrieval substrates and premise formation

684   The architecture has more than one retrieval implementation. This appendix separates lexical lookup,
685   embedding-neighbor search, graph navigation, and formal premise admission. It does not claim that
686   a single request automatically combines every index or that a learned fusion policy has been trained.
687   The relevant inspected sources are the legal sample builder, GraphRAG thin client, FAISS store, and
688   Hammer selector contract. [artifact S22,S34–S36]

689   N.1   BM25 in sample construction and corpus lookup

690   The sample builder invokes a BM25 frame selector to produce candidates and a selected ontology
691   frame. This is supervision provenance: a selected frame may be useful but is not automatically
692   a human-validated semantic label. The GraphRAG thin client is a different consumer of lexical
693   indexing. It tokenizes the query, locates relevant posting shards, and computes a term contribution of
694   the form
                                                           tf(t, d)(k1 + 1)
                                 s(t, d) = idf(t)                                   .                   (15)
                                                  tf(t, d) + k1 (1 − b + b |d|/|d|)
695   For one inspected posting schema, term frequency combines configured title and body weights.
696   However, a separate schema branch keyed by legal identifiers sums the supplied term frequencies
697   instead. Consequently, a response labeled “bm25” is not by itself evidence that every data schema
698   used the same full scoring formula. The manifest, posting schema, scoring branch, tokenizer, and
699   parameters belong in a reproducible run record. [artifact S34]
700   Lexical retrieval is useful for citations, names, identifiers, and distinctive vocabulary. It can also
701   miss paraphrases, rank a superficially matching exception above a controlling rule, or omit a relevant
702   provision. Its score supplies ordering, not entailment, completeness, or applicability. Frame-selection
703   errors can propagate into compiler-derived supervision unless independent checks expose them.

704   N.2   Vector indexing is separate from representation learning

705   The FAISS store maintains numerical indexes, per-collection metadata, and mappings between
706   external identifiers and internal index positions. Its inspected constructor requires real FAISS and
707   NumPy availability; legacy mock definitions in the file are not evidence of a successful real store
708   query. An evaluated route must identify the actual backend and status. [artifact S35]
709   Three operations must be recorded separately: producing a vector with a frozen or trained encoder;
710   fitting any index structures that a selected index type requires; and changing semantic model parame-
711   ters. The adaptive autoencoder’s embedding reconstruction is a fourth, separately specified numerical
712   operation. A published numerical vector does not prove that the corresponding source was formally
713   interpreted, and a store over vectors does not establish that those vectors came from this autoencoder.
714   The thin client declares a vector subcommand and contains an embedding helper, but its inspected
715   dispatch does not execute a vector query. Actual vector search must therefore be attributed to another
716   verified entry point rather than to that declaration. This is a route-specific gap, not a claim that the
717   repository lacks vector indexing. [artifact S34,S35]

718   N.3   Graphs connect evidence without silently adding axioms

719   The thin client can retrieve incoming/outgoing adjacency records and execute bounded walks over
720   CID-linked nodes. Its results retain neighbor identifiers and edge metadata. A graph can connect
721   a source passage to a definition, entity, related artifact, or dependency; an index edge does not
722   necessarily assert a logical implication. [artifact S34]
723   At the query boundary, lexical and vector hits can nominate seeds, graph navigation can expand their
724   context, and exact artifact identities can retrieve declarations. This is a proposed composition of the
725   available interfaces, not a measured fusion algorithm. A premise-admission stage must then inspect
726   source lineage, entity and temporal alignment, domain applicability, declared authority, and the
727   relevant interpretation. Keep “retrieved”, “admitted premise”, “assumed”, and “proved” as separate
728   states.
