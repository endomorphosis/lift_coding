478   E     Pipeline manifest, evaluation protocol, and result fields

479   One tested combination. Freeze the exact repository commit, loaded module paths, dirty overlays if
480   permitted, domain adapters, schema versions, source artifacts, vocabularies, review profiles, modality
481   profiles, compiler configuration, selected models/tokenizers, and solver/native checker versions. Do
482   not combine one branch’s compiler with a different branch’s results and call it one tested system.
483   This draft does not inspect unpushed local worktrees.

484   Three evaluation populations. Use: (i) controlled minimal pairs with independent expected
485   meanings; (ii) held-out natural documents, code, and traces with source-family/time splits and
486   reviewed entity mappings; and (iii) extracted-media examples only after the concrete extraction route
487   is installed and measured. Separate generator-authored examples from real held-out material. Keep
488   alternative correct formalizations and abstentions instead of forcing a unique string match.

489   Annotations and leakage. Freeze the intended propositions, logical profile, exceptions, temporal
490   intervals, ambiguous readings, source-map spans, and admissible assumptions before evaluating
491   model output. Annotators and proof scorers should not use candidate generation as their only oracle.
492   Source derivatives, paraphrases, translations, views, and adjacent sections can leak across splits.
493   Source-withheld realization must block original text, full source maps, file/path recovery, and access
494   to gold IR; it must not quietly retrieve the withheld source.
