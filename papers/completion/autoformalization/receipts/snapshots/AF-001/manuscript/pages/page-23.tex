\subsection*{O.1  Planning is deterministic until explicitly guided}
\noindent
The local LogicTactician validates the goal and policy, binds the configuration identity, validates\linebreak[4]
and partitions candidate sources, optionally applies pinned guidance, selects routes under budgets, and\linebreak[4]
decomposes declared proof gaps. Cycles cause abstention rather than a malformed plan. Excluded\linebreak[4]
sources retain a reason, and final plans carry input roots and guidance identities. The planner's own\linebreak[4]
implementation performs no proof, write, or network work. [artifact S38]\linebreak[4]
This is a domain-neutral orchestration layer. Its name should not be taken to imply implementation of\linebreak[4]
an external tactic-learning system. A separate executor is needed to realize a plan, and that execution\linebreak[4]
must supply its own artifacts and outcomes. Optional ranking and nomination have a narrower\linebreak[4]
contract than unconstrained generation of new assumptions or arbitrary executable tasks.\par
