\subsection*{5.2  The Tactician plans; the Hammer searches and reconstructs}
\noindent
The repository's LogicTactician deterministically orders caller-provided sources under an explicit\linebreak[4]
policy, records selected/excluded routes, creates an acyclic subgoal plan, and reports stop or abstention\linebreak[4]
conditions. Planning performs no proof, network work, or writes. Optional model guidance can\linebreak[4]
reorder or nominate admitted sources under a pinned digest, with deterministic fallback. This planner\linebreak[4]
is not itself a trained tactic synthesizer. [artifact S38]\linebreak[4]
The ITP Hammer selects premises, lowers a supported first-order-safe fragment to TPTP or SMT-\linebreak[4]
LIB, runs an allowed bounded solver portfolio, and attempts native reconstruction. Vampire/E are\linebreak[4]
first-order automation routes; Z3/cvc5 are SMT routes; Lean, Coq/Rocq, and Isabelle/HOL have\linebreak[4]
separate frontend/reconstruction contracts. A logic family specifies what a statement means; a prover\linebreak[4]
family specifies a computation over an admitted encoding. Unsupported dependent, higher-order, or\linebreak[4]
unresolved polymorphic constructs cannot be silently erased. External execution is opt-in, and native\linebreak[4]
availability must be established. [artifact S37]\linebreak[4]
Within this Hammer contract, solver output is a candidate until reconstruction is accepted by the\linebreak[4]
originating ITP's checker in its pinned environment. The deterministic premise baseline uses symbol,\linebreak[4]
type, import, and graph overlap, not a universal vector ranker. Its optional graph-selector artifact uses\linebreak[4]
hand-authored linear weights; the inspected release does not ship a genuinely trained premise-selector\linebreak[4]
checkpoint. Ranking machinery, training a ranker, and proving a goal are separate claims. [artifact\linebreak[4]
S36,S37]\par

\subsection*{5.3  Leanstral proposals and verified training feedback}
\noindent
Leanstral supplies advisory proof-engineering candidates, such as Lean edits or strategies. The\linebreak[4]
security lane requires the suggested proof to pass the Lean checker and rejects proof bypasses; a\linebreak[4]
model response does not replace source, runtime, or release evidence. The legal rollout separately\linebreak[4]
admits structured guidance through syntax, provenance, anti-copy, proof, and configured Lean-\linebreak[4]
reconstruction checks. Raw drafts never become reconstruction targets or trusted training labels.\linebreak[4]
Verification creates a new structured artifact rather than making the original prose authoritative.\linebreak[4]
[artifact S39,S40]\linebreak[4]
Together, retrieval nominates context, the Tactician organizes bounded work, Hammer and Leanstral\linebreak[4]
provide different proposal mechanisms, and checking determines the supported result. Admitted feed-\linebreak[4]
back can train isolated heads or identify a compiler defect; a versioned patch still needs independent\linebreak[4]
regression tests and rollout approval. This is not evidence that Leanstral's base weights are fine-tuned\linebreak[4]
by the adaptive autoencoder. The concrete contracts and return path are given in Appendix O.\par

\section*{6  How different IRs are used together}
\subsection*{6.1  Semantics are indexed by a profile}
\noindent
Each logical view has a signature, sentence language, semantic models, satisfaction relation, and a\linebreak[4]
supported query fragment. The common record layer does not force one implementation of these\linebreak[4]
components. A computable representation is not a universal decision procedure: queries may require\linebreak[4]
decidable fragments, bounded search, or explicit unknown/timeout outcomes. First-order and frame\linebreak[4]
views express objects and relations; event-calculus views express events, fluents, and change [6];\linebreak[4]
deontic views express normative force; temporal views bind trace/clock semantics; cognitive views\linebreak[4]
distinguish agent-indexed knowledge, belief, and intention. World, agent, time, action, and formula\linebreak[4]
categories cannot be exchanged merely because their identifiers are strings.\par
