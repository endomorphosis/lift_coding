\section*{9  Related work, limitations, and conclusion}
\noindent
Mathematical autoformalization [1, 2, 9] motivates learned statement generation, but our source\linebreak[4]
pipeline combines domain-specific parsing, structured targets, adaptive representation heads, and\linebreak[4]
checked compilation guidance. Learned perception plus symbolic execution is established in neural-\linebreak[4]
symbolic reasoning [8]; here provenance, modal scope, and separate proof-feedback objectives are\linebreak[4]
material. We do not claim to originate multi-logic interoperability: institutions formalize signatures,\linebreak[4]
models, and satisfaction [4], while LogiKEy combines normative logics by HOL embedding [3].\linebreak[4]
Frame logic, event calculus, and hammer reconstruction are established foundations [5, 6, 10].\linebreak[4]
The main limitations are teacher bias, possible sample-memory shortcuts, repeated validation-set\linebreak[4]
selection, unmeasured checkpoint activation, dynamic source behavior, lossy legacy translations,\linebreak[4]
and bounded backend coverage. The proof space is neither a universal latent space nor a universal\linebreak[4]
decision procedure. A trained view classifier, valid source map, or high reconstruction score does not\linebreak[4]
by itself establish faithful interpretation. Result kinds remain distinct: theorem proof, satisfiability,\linebreak[4]
finite runtime observation, evidence readiness, and policy approval. [artifact S19]\linebreak[4]
The methodology is best understood as a coupled learning and compilation system: symbolic structure\linebreak[4]
supplies auditable targets; trainable shared representations expose systematic errors; constrained\linebreak[4]
proposals and fixed-canary admission improve the compiler-facing path; and modality-aware views\linebreak[4]
support explicitly justified reasoning. The next result to establish is whether this cycle improves\linebreak[4]
independent source fidelity and useful cross-view proof coverage on genuinely unseen material, at a\linebreak[4]
favorable total cost.\par
