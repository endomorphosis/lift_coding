\paragraph{Empirical scope.}
We evaluate automated structural and execution properties of the inspected
pipeline: typed elaboration, declared compiler contracts, reconstruction
agreement labeled by its producer, training and checkpoint updates with exact
split/seed/cost bindings, and native checker acceptance of an exact declared
formal goal under a pinned checker identity. Each reported quantity must carry
its own execution record, population, seed, source, goal, premises, checker and
resource bindings. A checker-accepted formal statement is not evidence that the
statement faithfully captures the original natural-language source. Automatic
teacher agreement and round-trip agreement are reported as such and are not
original-source semantic gold.

(AF-006 twelve reconstructed checks; AF-015 property-specific Boolean/trace

receipt names the goal, premises, checker digest and outcome, including
explicit failures (AF-027 Lean identity success, Lean \texttt{False:=trivial}

unavailable). Learned updates and teacher diagnostics remain bound to the

1913-unit no-run denominator; unrun and unavailable are not measured zeros.

Development, fixed-canary and constructed results must not be presented as
untouched final-distribution performance.
\paragraph{Unperformed human evaluation.}
No independent human semantic-fidelity study, inter-annotator agreement study,
or adjudication was performed for this manuscript. Their proposed protocols
are retained as unexecuted work and supply no empirical score. The prepared
100-unit candidate-blind sample across 20 groups remains blank; zero
agreement is an inventory of missing records, not a reliability statistic.
Any optional author comments, if supplied, are descriptive non-independent
feedback with author provenance, remain outside gold and scored metrics, and
are not required for manuscript or task completion. The manuscript's
supported contribution is limited to the automated evidence actually
reported; source-semantic fidelity and its transfer remain unmeasured.
