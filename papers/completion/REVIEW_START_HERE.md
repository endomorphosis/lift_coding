# Author proofreading

Start with the three [final manuscript PDFs and supplements](DELIVERY.md). Their task workflows are complete, all three abstracts are registered, and no outside reviewer or returned annotation packet is required.

For an author pass:

1. Read the exact delivered PDFs, including limitations, disclosures and checklists.
2. Keep measured automated results separate from unmeasured human fidelity, agreement and expert time. The Supervision comparison is limited to eight historical families and A/B cold trials.
3. Keep any edited draft separate and record its new file hashes.
4. Read the manuscript and complete the portal's required author acknowledgments when making the actual submission. These are genuine author responsibilities; they do not block completion of this local manuscript handoff. No submission has been performed by this workflow.

Optional author feedback is descriptive feedback, not independent human evaluation. Earlier unperformed reviewer instructions are preserved as [historical material](history/pre_final_delivery_v1/REVIEW_START_HERE.md); they are not current assignments or completion prerequisites.
