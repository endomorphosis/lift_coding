\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

Persistent semantic state, obligation-guided reasoning, evidence reuse and
accepted-state publication can avoid repeated work under declared acceptance
conditions. Constructed symbolic closures avoid model calls, but simple controls
match some memory benefits and priming can outweigh validation reuse. Native
publication and proofs bind artifacts without proving arbitrary execution truth.
Neither natural repository comparison completes a trajectory or establishes a
general efficiency advantage; additive context remains unfavorable. Whole-system
token savings still require matched, independently scored trajectories, mechanism
ablations and complete setup, failure and recovery accounting.
