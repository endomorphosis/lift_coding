The v4 qualification attempted the larger full-codec path under a four-GiB
address-space ceiling and failed on an allocation. V5 uses the exact C native

`native_b.py` implements actual typed-deontic compilation followed by the native
source-withheld decompiler and recompilation. Its added vocabulary producer is
an explicit source-only lexical projection: contiguous ngrams of length 1–5 for
actors, objects, and qualifiers; unigrams for actions; shortest spans first;
maximum 1,000 atoms per category; first 4,096 source characters; five-second
per-record budget. Native policy and CID checks are unchanged. L1/L2 equality
measures structural cycle stability and never establishes fidelity to the law.

The first B selection pilot erroneously allowed 2,048 vocabulary entries,
exceeding the native 1,000-entry contract on three sources. Those errors remain
in the first pilot. The corrected v2 selection run has 15 records: 11 explicit
abstentions, two native empty-output failures, and two timeouts, with no harness
errors. The six constructed controls under the final profile include four
successful pipelines, one abstention, and one empty-output failure. Three of the
four successful pipelines preserve L1 exactly; the temporal/exception control
exposes genuine structural round-trip drift.

All of these are automated implementation and experiment results. Independent
human legal/source-fidelity annotations remain unmeasured and were never used
as an approval gate for this engineering work.
