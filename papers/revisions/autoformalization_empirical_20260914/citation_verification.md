## DSP author-order discrepancy

The official ICLR event HTML, including its structured author data, lists Jiang, Welleck, Zhou, **Lacroix, Liu, Li, Jamnik**, Lample, Wu. The accepted PDF hosted by Cambridge lists Jiang, Welleck, Zhou, **Li, Liu, Jamnik, Lacroix**, Lample, Wu, reading the three author rows from left to right. The old bibliography instead ended in Wu, Lample, consistent with the arXiv metadata rather than either conference representation. The entry now follows the official conference index and records the discrepancy here rather than silently combining versions. Direct OpenReview access returned a browser-verification page during this check; the author sequence was independently obtained from the official ICLR event page, not claimed as a freshly retrieved OpenReview API result.
