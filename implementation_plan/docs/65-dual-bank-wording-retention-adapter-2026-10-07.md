## Completed adapter

The source adapter composes the two original private renderer profiles and
source-envelope validators. It retains control13 and balanced16 exclusions,
separate 180-clause bank records and two immutable tensor handles. Paired sampling
is adopted without replacing or copying model/tensor handles; original receipts
stay intact. Every global update selects one bank and its own local ordinal,
with one original six-clause/full 32 CE forward. Retry observations reuse the draw.

The inherited trainer still owns original decoder/count streams, objective,
optimizer, clipping, scheduler and checkpoint selection. Its actual commit
ledger supplies truthful dual-bank summaries: 85 updates/510 presentations per bank,
255 per actual template and 340 per modality across 170 updates. Obsolete single-bank
counts remain labeled inapplicable; prepared schedules or loss forwards never
become optimizer commits. Private import hooks preserve other imports and the
omitted/None path. Genuine cache-preparation timeouts remain zero-commit partial
reports.

All 82 new controls and 54 existing controls pass: 136 distinct cases without failures
or skips. Independent review approved handle ownership, receipt lineage,
schema/weight/gradient consistency and timeout handling. A separate real-asset
probe reproduced the schedule from the actual retained source envelopes without
Torch during preparation. It restored the original selected 384D auxiliary
checkpoint and executed 170 detached source-head forwards, covering all 360 source
clauses with 1,020 observations and zero optimizer commits. Every sampled modality
argmax was correct; this is an observation on the already-trained parent.
Weights, source bytes and RNG remained unchanged. The
[retained evidence](../../artifacts/dual-bank-wording-adapter-20261007/README.md)
separates synthetic controls, actual-asset probes and independent reviews.
