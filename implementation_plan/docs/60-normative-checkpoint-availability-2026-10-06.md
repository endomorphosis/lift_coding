# Normative checkpoints available for the next reconstruction work

The eight completed normative checkpoint serializations are now published and
registered through the genuine `ipfs_accelerate_py` ModelManager. They represent
four unique trained tensor endpoints: zero and auxiliary arms at 384D and 768D,
each with distinct selected and last-attempt containers. Original saved bytes,
codec, recipe and immediate-parent/donor identities are preserved. The selected
and last-attempt containers are aliases, not independent teachers or replications.

The selected store `/home/barberb/lift_coding/external/ipfs_accelerate/model_manager.duckdb`
grew from 670 to 678 records. Full native row/schema comparisons preserve every
prior metadata and activity field. Cold native reads, a genuine manager reload
and exact ten-selector IR catalog resolution verify all eight bindings. Repeating
the operation finds all eight and performs no manager construction or store
write. Another running service is not automatically refreshed; new task contexts
must bind the current catalog generation through normal preparation.

## Combine the latest other-agent result

The subsequently published [retention observation](https://github.com/endomorphosis/ipfs_datasets_py/blob/1ff99942caf57d8dc82cd3ddbe8df962752da7aa/docs/autoencoders/normative_wording_retention.md)
joins all four selected state and tensor identities to these same registered
assets. Its exposed 48-paragraph panel gives 384D exact IR 20→31/48 and 768D
46→48/48, with lower token CE at both widths. This supplements the separate
60-row wording panel, where 384D improves 55→60/60 while 768D remains 60/60
with slightly worse auxiliary CE. Counts, losses and source populations remain
separate. No new fitting or numerical evaluation ran in this availability work.

The 384D auxiliary still has 21 incorrect generated modality values and one
incorrect action. At two modality sites the source-head argmax is correct while
the generated value is wrong. These are diagnostic inputs for controlled tests;
they do not establish a causal recurrent effect or justify forced replacements.
The 768D auxiliary is exact on this exposed panel's 180 rules. Qualifiers are
empty, meaning exposure is known, and general legal fidelity is unmeasured.
Original-text reconstruction remains a separate task from canonical IR exactness.
