The selected v2 has 64/64 exact authored positive outputs on a fresh final set
and 55/64 learned unsupported-profile refusals. Seven further negatives are
incidentally blocked and two emit unsupported requests. Published v1 has 40/64
exact positives and nineteen unsupported emissions on the same panel, under a

there is no demonstrated real-law formalization coverage or reviewed legal
accuracy. The final authored panel is now exposed.

The [shared findings](../../artifacts/autoencoder-consolidation-20261006-02/scientific-integration-findings.md)
and [datasets guide](../../external/ipfs_datasets/docs/autoencoders/grouped_legal_decoders.md)
connect the head to existing statement-scope, lane-bundle, label-review, masked
contrastive and native-family owners. Those engineering contracts are already
implemented; authentic reviewed/admitted semantic pairs and richer compatible
representations remain missing. Do not rebuild a second admission or alignment
subsystem, relabel compiler/cache proposals as gold, or drop qualifiers to fit a
short grammar.

The next training work should adapt provenance-bound normative TRAIN wording to
the grouped output contract, preserve roles/objects/qualifier attachments and
parent split groups, and reserve new final sources. Actual 8D/384D/768D
conditioning needs its own encoder/vector lineage and matched raw/PCA/AE/source
controls. The grouped profile currently covers only deontic FOL; broad Legal
family requirements and reviewed legal scope interpretation remain open.
