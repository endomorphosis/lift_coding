## Bootstrap, refill, and terminal policy

- Bootstrap task PCPR-004 is the sole initial schedulable task and readiness frontier.
- The 66 supplied blueprint packages remain non-executable until PCPR-004 emits a fresh admitted direct-handoff qualification receipt and the high-level objective is submitted through that path.
- The direct objective may materialize all 66 packages as its bounded initial plan because the maximum initial population is 80.
- Later automatic refills are append-only, content-addressed, deduplicated, evidence-bound, limited to 12 tasks per epoch, 20 epochs, and 140 total tasks.
- At least 30 percent of token and compute budgets remains reserved for final validation.
- Every escalation has a typed unresolved question; no model call occurs when its answer cannot change an admissible decision.
- Missing providers, solvers, hardware, drivers, permissions, and signing authority produce typed dispositions or operator gates, not indefinite blocking.
- Release decision task PCPR-094 is the release-decision barrier. PCPR-096 may recommend a pilot but cannot create it.
- Empty queue, board completion, model assertion, present CID, stored proof, or hermetic fixture is never root completion evidence.
