This is a read-only review of published reports at workspace `origin/main`
`3b0162bebe0b7a09727081cb8c5f5c15378caba1` and datasets `origin/main`
`5171a632c6b9f0ecb2939d29d2ad74992cbfeb11`. It executes no model, encoder,
training, proof, or download. [The evidence matrix](evidence-matrix.json) binds
16 representative findings to exact Git commits, report paths, byte sizes and
SHA-256 hashes. All 16 commits are ancestors of the reviewed remote main.
A Git ancestor check establishes reachability, not retention of all historical
files or integration of every executable caller; the parent review audits those
separately.

The work is complementary, with four different numerical objectives. The
preserved sparse linguistic runtime reconstructs feature vectors; the new
source-only MLPs compress native source vectors; the formula sidecars generate
restricted authored Legal IR; and native family autoencoders reconstruct
compiler-prepared projection features. Their widths, losses and checkpoint
schemas must not be conflated. The learned Security production decoder is a
separate bounded parser-scaffolded grammar head. None can inherit another
owner's source-fidelity or Lake evidence by sharing an `autoencoder` label.

The October 5 source-only studies provide a useful lower bound on representation
choices. Expanded DEV mean MSE fell 82.36%, 80.94% and 77.03% for the 8D, 384D
and 768D MLPs compared with older same-seed models on the new cohort. Every
new model still trails TRAIN-only PCA. The 32/64 bottlenecks are also above the
centered TRAIN rank of 31. These outcomes measure cohort adaptation and native
vector geometry, not semantic quality or an optimal representation width.
[Published numerical scalars](published-scalars.json) retain the exact means.

The frozen downstream experiment makes the missing bridge concrete. Raw,
PCA-reconstructed and AE-reconstructed 768D vectors produce identical generated
IR/status/reason on all 192 paired source/seed occurrences, under both greedy
and joint policies. Those inverse reconstructions remain full 768D inputs to
unchanged raw-trained decoder weights. This does not compare equally trained
conditioner-specific decoders, lower-dimensional latent storage, or compression
latency. Enabled zero vectors retain affine FiLM biases; disabling the gate
removes its residual, so the controls must remain separate.

Bounded joint span selection resolves many overlap abstentions: 10→183/192
proposals on the raw arm, with all ten old proposals unchanged. It reuses each
native forward, keeps the original modality/presence decisions, and adds a
Top-16/beam-64 search. Every search prunes some candidates or states. The result
is a promising proposal policy, with actor, modality, scope and qualifier errors
still visible; it establishes neither semantic improvement nor a global optimum.
The six-condition follow-up repeats the same sources/checkpoints and is a
mechanism control, not another independent fidelity cohort.

The latest paraphrase-modality study likewise narrows the next hypothesis. Its
full 180-clause TRAIN readout was already 180/180 before fitting. Additional CE
supervision reduced uncertainty on those training clauses and reduced exposed
wording CE by 6.7%/10.6%, but exact reconstruction fell 20→19 at 384D and stayed
46/48 at 768D. Raising that saturated auxiliary alone is unsupported. Wording-
specific modality errors and the recurrent contribution deserve attention while
preserving the original development selection gate.

The integration sequence should first register these distinct owners and their
source/target contracts, then admit independently reviewed source/formal pairs
covering actor/action/object roles, negation, modality, qualifier attachment,
optional presence, context, operators and multiple-rule associations. The same
pairs can drive repaired production heads and source/formal alignment without
turning compiler agreement into gold labels. Only after decoder grammar and
TRAIN-pair admission exist should matched raw/PCA/AE and source-only decoders
be trained with equal initialization, update and decoding budgets. True reduced
latents and 4096D capacity need separate measured cost controls.

Reuse full atom support, family-specific semantic owners, solver routing and
real `lake build <Lib>` gates. Complete-feature timing shows that numerical
refinement used only 7.55 of 315.04 seconds of full fitting, so reducing repeated
native validation/preparation is a stronger throughput hypothesis than claiming
a new optimizer from small CE differences. Any cache reuse must bind the exact
source generation, target, producer and policy, and must retain required live
admission checks.

Keep every repeated baseline and rejected endpoint for audit, but count them
as controls. `8c02d655...` and `3b39519d...` have identical joint-conditioning
artifact trees, verified in [the publication comparison](duplicate-publications.json),
and describe one executed study. R6 and v3 wording sets have already influenced
iterations; two seeds produce repeated predictions of the same examples. The
4096D 2-row TRAIN fit, 12-row development result, and initial readiness controls
are different cohorts and profiles, not inconsistent measurements.

Native IntentIR/UIUXIR/SecurityIR/LegalIR builds exist in the integration work;
the floor is broader than only Legal. Their source-specific partial results do
not establish all 40 families or source truth. Wrong UI predictions have passed
Lake, underscoring the need for a separate source-fidelity measurement. The
restricted October 4 decoder studies run no Lake build and promote no checkpoint.
Only the actual required `lake build <Lib>` can supply Lean admission. The
Constitution remains unformalized.
