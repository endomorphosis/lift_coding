# Next experiments for formal logic alignment

Date: 2026-10-04. This stage audits saved artifacts and completes the next experiment designs. It executes no model, new embedding, optimizer, prover, review, label admission or service operation.

The [comprehensive improvement plan](../../implementation_plan/docs/49-autoformalization-joint-embeddings-improvement-plan-2026-10-03.md) retains all three native source lanes, the retained decoder checkout, the current logic-family/prover routes and separate Leanstral generation and embedding roles. Following [ProofBridge v3](https://arxiv.org/html/2510.15681v3), the proposed extension aligns source, formal statements and verified proof views, retrieves compatible demonstrations and uses bounded repair. Our broader family routing and source-fidelity gates are extensions of that research approach. Proof validity still concerns the encoded statement; independent review must establish whether it preserves the source.

The next practical work has three parts: establish usable semantic labels, reuse existing projection heads under an explicit diagnostic or semantic training policy, and qualify Leanstral's observed embedding route before collecting comparison vectors.

The current checkpoint owner accepts native384 input and shared dimensions 384 or 512; its source profile matches the saved GTE-small vectors. Preserve raw 8D spaCy, 384D GTE-small and 768D multilingual GTE controls and their independent autoencoder views. New 8D/768D projection profiles must bind their own producer identities.

The current TRAIN manifest contains 16 distinct typed proposals, 124 anchors and four author groups. Its masks permit weak decoder fitting while contrastive, strong semantic, proof and fidelity supervision remain zero. Author groups supply no equivalence relation. Existing action-based positive and hard-negative heuristics select no pairs. Eight directed pairs share core facets while differing in qualifiers; these are optional weak structural contrasts whose semantic non-equivalence has not been independently established.

The proposed diagnostic matrix uses three seeds, two shared widths and uniform versus weak qualifier weighting: 12 arms with 80 fixed steps each, or 960 requested updates. A separately versioned weak diagnostic policy is required before any fit, or the experiment can wait for independently admitted labels. Initial states are matched within seed/width, TRAIN alone fits the 27D formal features, and no DEV selection occurs. Target-free rankings are saved before optional authored scoring. Fixed candidate-pool coverage and all-request denominators remain visible.

This design has not run. It proposes a fresh CPU worker, disabled CUDA, one thread, 180-second wall/CPU ceilings and a measured 2 GiB RSS envelope. Current negative weights cannot exclude unknown pairs; that requires a versioned loss extension. The bag-of-facets view also loses operators and multiple-rule scope. Independently reviewed multi-positive training needs a richer formal view and reviewed equivalence relationships before it can support semantic conclusions.

## Actual Leanstral embedding evidence

The pinned backend exposes final normalized hidden activations before its 131,072-wide vocabulary head. This verifies a numerical hidden-vector route for the inspected asset, using raw text, last-token pooling and L2 normalization.

The saved capability check also records a material failure: single-versus-batch L2 difference is **0.145932**, above the registered **0.001** tolerance. A/B/A replay is exact, but fixed-batch repeatability does not establish invariance to peers, position, order, padding or slot allocation. The cause is unestablished. Retain the batching/workload identity with these cached vectors until that behavior is qualified. Requested context is 8,192 globally, with **512 tokens per slot** across 16 slots; long-input behavior and mean pooling were not exercised.

The benchmark reports a 249.23-second cold load and median 15.43 seconds per warm 800-source pass, approximately 51.85 sources per second. Warm timings include tokenization, HTTP inference, validation and cache erasure but exclude output-file writing. These are historical throughput observations, not a new resource measurement or retrieval-quality result. No exact source text joins the old 34 requests, which contain 33 distinct source strings, or the new 64-source packet. The saved cache cannot supply their comparison vectors.

The audit checks the executed producer export, nine runtime binaries, four backend source files and saved workload/vector bindings. Current producer changes concern cleanup/restoration safeguards; the inference measurement body is unchanged. The inspected model's full SHA remains manifest-declared. Matching metadata-prefix and runtime pins do not independently authenticate every weight byte or establish a distinct customized fine-tuned checkpoint.

The historical benchmark temporarily replaces the managed generation service and lacks the common runner's full lock/admission envelope. The next runtime owner should integrate bounded embedding capability with those controls. Before collecting new panel vectors, predeclare single/batch-size, peer, order, position, padding, cache and overlength checks; bind actual forwarded token/pooling identities or record unavailable evidence. Evaluate mean pooling as its own complete-sequence profile. Retrieval quality and downstream source fidelity follow capability qualification.
