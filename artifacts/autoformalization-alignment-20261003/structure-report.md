# Structural declaration features and richer review preparation

The new open-vocabulary features retain complete canonical rules and their
qualifier relationships. The measured artifacts reconstruct all 24 authored
positive declarations, all 22 saved parser outputs, and six manually authored
structural challenges exactly. This establishes preservation of normalized
seven-facet Legal IR; independent correspondence to source meaning remains
unavailable. Statement generation stays at the prior 22/24 authored matches.

The stage also prepares 34 blank source/context review items. It creates no
human reviews, identity attestations, native proofs, or model embeddings.

For the same 24 positive references, the frozen 22D flat codec has 18 distinct
vectors and six distinct-target collision pairs. The frozen richer 27D codec
already distinguishes all 24, but its shared unknown bucket merges the two
newly authored unseen-condition challenge declarations. The exact structural
dictionary retains those different labels without fitting a vocabulary.

Two further challenge pairs test associations among multiple rules. Swapping
conditions and exceptions between two rules with the same core leaves typed
atom counts and core-associated qualifier counts unchanged. Swapping
conditions between different actors leaves the global atom bag unchanged.
Complete-rule descriptors distinguish both changes. Existing flat codecs
support only single-rule declarations, so those multi-rule cases are marked
inapplicable rather than assigned an invented pooled vector.

The dictionary retains opaque atom spelling, typed facets, complete rule
coassociation, and duplicate rule occurrences. It follows the existing owner's
sorted rules and sorted unique qualifier lists. Reconstruction does not retain
original ordering or repeated qualifier entries, infer the meaning inside
atoms, or add quantified-variable support.

The reference scope contains 164 distinct feature names. The fixed coordinate
recipe sums positive integer counts and records every modulo bucket
assignment. Cross-declaration token collisions and equal whole declaration
vectors are separate measurements. Zero observed vector collisions on this
small panel does not establish general injectivity. The exact dictionary,
rather than the hashed counts, supplies reconstruction. SHA-based descriptor
identity also relies on its declared cryptographic assumption.

These 2048D/4096D formal features are separate from the 8D spaCy, 384D GTE, and
768D multilingual GTE source inputs and from learned projection dimensions.
No autoencoder was retrained and no Leanstral hidden states were extracted.

## Review handoff

Send reviewers only [reviewer_items.json](structure-01/reviewer_items.json) and
[reviewer_manifest.json](structure-01/reviewer_manifest.json). They contain
exact source text, separately supplied context, input-based pseudonyms, and
blank interpretation fields. Proposed references, candidates, partitions,
case types, and compiler outcomes are withheld. The two context assumptions
remain distinct inputs; automated context resolution remains unavailable.

Keep the organizer files and full bundle private. Audience-specific filenames
do not enforce access controls. All 34 items remain pending; zero independent
reviews or authenticated attestations are recorded. The prior 40-item review
schema cannot be reused for this richer packet. A future submission-admission
interface must preserve richer free-form scope and ambiguity annotations.
