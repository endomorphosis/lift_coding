Source conditioning/adaptation on permitted pre-task repository files is a disclosed transductive protocol, not zero-shot unseen-repository evaluation. Keep source inventories, public task exposure, authored operator history, clone controls and all failed training attempts visible. A public smoke/evaluation filename neither proves leakage nor establishes a clean holdout; actual permitted-source provenance must decide it. No hidden grader, reference fix, final holdout outcome or expected patch was used for new training or selection in this audit, because no such training occurred.

The checkpoint/IR domain heads remain separate. T1 local source-learning updates do not train IntentIR, LegalIR or UI/UX semantics by implication. A deterministic training target is not substituted for a wrong learned prediction. Preparation, local inference, failed attempts, requalification and amortization must be accounted separately in any future deployment comparison.
